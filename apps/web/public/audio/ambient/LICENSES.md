# Ambient recordings

These recordings are sourced from Blanket revision
`9d229d2be7cb6619135d55ff9e49926e40298686` (retrieved 2026-09-22).
For browser compatibility, the Ogg recordings were transcoded to MP3 with
FFmpeg/libmp3lame quality 5. No other edits were made.
`coffee-shop.ogg` was renamed to `cafe.mp3`.
